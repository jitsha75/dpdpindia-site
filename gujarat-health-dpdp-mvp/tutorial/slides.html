The end-to-end workflow

1Discover

Find every system, share and vendor holding patient data

Analyst, HMIS cell, CDHOs
2Declare

Purpose, legal basis (s.7 or consent) and retention per system

Scheme owners; DPO approves
3Inform

Notices in Gujarati, Hindi, English; consent where required

IEC cell, facility stewards
4Control vendors

Contract scope monitored; notices drafted and approved

Contracts + DPO
5Serve rights

Access, correction, erasure, grievance within timelines

Rights cell, officers
6Respond

Breach triage; Board report in 72 h; CERT-In in 6 h

CDHO first responder; DPO
7Retain & erase

Keep what the law requires; erase the rest with proof

System owners; DPO approves
8Prove

Tamper-evident ledger for the Board, CAG and courts

Internal audit
Every action in steps 1–7 writes evidence to step 8. The cycle repeats as systems, vendors and schemes change.
Each screen in the platform maps to one step3

Presenting well

  1. Lead with Gujarat's own cases and the 13 May 2027 deadline; keep features in the background.
  2. Let an official drive one action: decrypt a record, or approve a deletion as the DPO.
  3. End on Governance & roadmap with a concrete first step: name the State DPO and the district nodal officers.

All patient data in the demo is synthetic. Vendor names marked "(demo)" stand for categories of processor.
