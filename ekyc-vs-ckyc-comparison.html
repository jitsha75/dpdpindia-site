Aadhaar eKYC vs CKYC
— What's the Difference, and When Does Each Apply?

eKYC and CKYC are often used interchangeably — they are not the same system. eKYC verifies who a customer is, live, through UIDAI. CKYC stores that verification once and makes it reusable, through CERSAI. This guide lays out the difference dimension by dimension, updated for CKYC 2.0.

In practice the two are complementary stages of one customer journey: eKYC answers "is this person who they claim to be, right now" at first onboarding; CKYC answers "has this person already completed KYC somewhere I can trust, so I don't need to repeat the exercise." Understanding which system you are actually dealing with — and when — determines whether your organisation needs a UIDAI AUA/KUA agreement at all.

Section 01

The Fundamental Distinction

Aadhaar eKYC — UIDAI / AUA-KUA
A live verification service
A real-time identity verification and data-sharing service run by UIDAI, accessed only via AUA/KUA/Sub-KUA agreements. Every eKYC pull is a fresh Aadhaar authentication event — not reusable across institutions.
CKYC — CERSAI / CKYCR 2.0
A shared, persistent registry
A stored, retrievable record of completed KYC — built from any valid method (Aadhaar eKYC, V-CIP, physical documents) — accessible to any Reporting Entity with the customer's OTP consent. Designed for reuse: one KIN serves every RE the customer subsequently deals with.

Section 03

When Each Applies

🆕
Brand-new customer, no KIN
eKYC

No CKYC record exists yet. The institution must verify identity from scratch — Aadhaar eKYC (via AUA/KUA/Sub-KUA), V-CIP, or physical documents.

First-time verification — CKYC has nothing to reuse yet
🔁
Existing customer, new institution
CKYC

Customer already banks/invests/insures elsewhere. New institution searches CKYCR by PAN/KIN/mobile, gets OTP consent, downloads the record — no fresh eKYC needed.

Reuse — the entire point of CKYC 2.0's design
🧾
Regulatory record-keeping
CKYC

Every RE must upload every completed KYC (however sourced) to CERSAI within 3 working days — this is a standing PMLA obligation, independent of how the identity was originally verified.

PMLA Rule 9(1A) — mandatory upload, not optional
🪪
Live biometric assurance needed
eKYC

High-assurance use cases (large-value account opening, EDD for high-risk customers) may still require a fresh biometric Aadhaar eKYC even if a CKYC record exists, per the institution's own risk policy.

CKYC record currency/assurance may not meet EDD bar
Section 04

The Bottom Line

One sentence each

eKYC is how an institution first confirms who you are, using UIDAI's live Aadhaar authentication through a licensed AUA/KUA. CKYC is where that confirmation gets stored — once, centrally, at CERSAI — so every other bank, NBFC, insurer, or broker you deal with afterward can reuse it with your consent instead of repeating the exercise. CKYC 2.0 (live from 1 August 2026) makes that reuse faster to find, harder to fake, and more visible to you every time it happens.
